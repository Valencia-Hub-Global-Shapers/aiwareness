01What is AIwareness?

AIwareness is a two-phase web tool. First, a quick right or wrong quiz asking "real or AI-generated?", drawing from a shared image bank. Then, a training phase that walks back through each image, in each hub's own language, explaining what gives away AI-generated content.

It is open to anyone, of any age, and it asks for birth year to see how accuracy varies across generations. Hubs are encouraged to bring it beyond Shapers, into their wider local community. Global Shapers Valencia built the app and handles all deployment, the database, and maintenance. Your hub only contributes content.

What we need from your hub
  • Images. Pick from our shared image bank, or send us new real and AI-generated photos for your hub.
  • A short template filled in. Your hub's language, city, chosen images, and local explanations.
  • We handle all the technical setup for you.
What your hub gets in return
  • Your city in the app's hub selector. Your community takes the quiz in their own language, Shapers and beyond.
  • A ready-to-use AI-literacy activity for events and workshops, with zero infrastructure cost. Valencia Hub hosts everything on a free-tier plan.
  • Cross-hub statistics. Your results feed into accuracy data by country, hub, and age group, plus direct support throughout onboarding.
